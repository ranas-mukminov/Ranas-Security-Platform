# Security policy

ScopeProof Lab v0.1 is an **offline synthetic laboratory**, not a real-target scanner or an authorization system. Its JSON scope record is self-declared and is not legal proof of permission.

For potentially sensitive bugs, use the repository's private **Security → Report a vulnerability** function if enabled. If unavailable, use a maintainer contact from the linked [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ranas-mukminov) and do not publish customer data, passwords, private IPs, tokens or exploit material.

Never test systems you do not own or lack explicit written authorization to assess. Do not run this demonstration against external organizations or submit their infrastructure to automated agents.

We do not promise that this demo detects all vulnerabilities. Do not treat its SHA-256 evidence chain as a digital signature, legally trusted timestamp, or proof of author identity.
